Questions

Straight answers

Do I need full coverage in Arizona?
Arizona requires liability only, but if you have a loan or lease the lender will require collision and comprehensive. We will tell you exactly what is required versus optional.
Will my rate go up if I file a claim?
Possibly, depending on fault and your carrier. That is exactly why we review whether to file on smaller incidents.
Can you bundle auto with home?
Yes, and it is the fastest discount most clients get. We quote both together so you see the bundle price, not a guess.
