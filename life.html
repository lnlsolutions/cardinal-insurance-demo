Coverage

What we write

Term life

Level premiums for 10, 15, 20 or 30 years. The most coverage for the lowest cost during the years your family depends on your income.

Whole life

Permanent coverage with cash value, for estate planning and final expenses that never expire.

Final expense

Smaller policies for funeral and medical bills, simplified issue with no medical exam for most applicants.

No-exam options

Available for many applicants under set age and coverage limits, usually decided in days rather than weeks.

Beneficiary check

Questions

Straight answers

How much life insurance do I need?
A common starting point is 10 to 12 times income plus debts and education costs, minus existing coverage. We will run your actual numbers.
Is the medical exam required?
Not always. Simplified issue products skip the exam for many applicants, though the coverage amounts are lower.
Can I be declined?
Some conditions make standard coverage harder, but there are carriers that specialize. It is worth asking before assuming no.
