Questions

What people ask before they switch

Will switching actually save me money?
Sometimes, not always. We compare like-for-like coverage, not just price, because a cheaper policy with lower limits is not a saving. If your current policy is already competitive we will tell you.
Do you handle claims or just sell policies?
We file the claim with you and stay on it. You get a real person in Gilbert, not a national call center script.
Can you insure a business as well as my home and cars?
Yes. Commercial auto, BOP, general liability, workers comp and bonds, often bundled with your personal lines for a discount.
What if I have a bad driving record or a prior claim?
We work with several carriers that specialize in non-standard risks, so a ticket or a lapse does not automatically mean the state pool.
