Questions

Straight answers

Do I need a BOP or a general liability policy?
If you have a location and business property, a BOP usually bundles both for less. If you are service-only and mobile, standalone general liability may fit better.
Can you cover subcontracted labor?
It depends on how you use subs. This is one of the biggest gaps we find, and it is worth ten minutes to get right.
How fast can you issue a certificate?
Usually same day for existing clients, which matters when a GC is waiting on you.
