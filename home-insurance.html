Questions

Straight answers

Is flood covered by my homeowners policy?
No. Flood is almost always a separate policy, and there is usually a waiting period, so it is worth checking before storm season.
How much dwelling coverage do I need?
Rebuild cost, not market value or your purchase price. We calculate it from square footage, finishes and current local construction costs.
What happens if I remodel?
Tell us. Additions, pools and upgraded finishes change replacement cost, and underinsuring after a remodel is one of the most common claim problems.

Is your home underinsured?

Bring your current declarations page and we will compare coverage line by line, free.
